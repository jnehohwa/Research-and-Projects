    1. Assignment Overview

    The rapid growth of digital learning, professional certification programmes and corporate skills development initiatives has
    transformed the education and training sector. Organisations now serve large numbers of learners across multiple locations
    while providing online enrolment, assessment services, learner support and operational reporting. As demand for these
    services continues to increase, organisations require enterprise applications that can process large volumes of transactions
    e"ciently, support multiple users simultaneously and remain maintainable as business requirements evolve.

    Many training providers continue to rely on ageing or monolithic systems that were originally designed for smaller user
    populations. As these systems expand, operational challenges begin to emerge. Registration processes become slower
    during peak enrolment periods, reporting functions consume increasing amounts of system resources, support requests
    accumulate faster than they can be processed and application maintenance becomes increasingly di"cult. In many cases,
    poor scalability, ine#ective concurrency management and tightly coupled system components result in reduced
    performance, limited flexibility and a poor user experience.

    The organisation in this scenario provides professional certification programmes, short learning programmes and corporate
    training courses to thousands of learners across South Africa. The organisation currently uses several disconnected systems
    to manage learner registrations, course enrolments, assessments, support requests and reporting activities. As learner
    numbers continue to grow, management has identified concerns relating to application performance, duplicated
    information, ine"cient workflows and the inability to scale individual components independently.

    Management has therefore commissioned the development of a Python-based enterprise application prototype that will
    serve as a centralised platform for managing learner information, registrations, assessments, support requests and
    operational reporting. The solution must demonstrate how enterprise programming principles can be applied to improve
    scalability, maintainability, performance and reliability within a real-world environment.

    The proposed system must support:

         learner registration and management
         course and assessment management
         support ticket creation and tracking
         concurrent registration processing
         enterprise reporting and monitoring
         performance optimisation
         automated testing
         application profiling
         error logging and diagnostics
         future migration to a microservices architecture

    You are required to design and develop a Python-based enterprise application prototype that demonstrates the practical
    application of enterprise programming concepts. Your solution must reflect sound architectural decisions, appropriate use
    of design patterns, e#ective concurrency management, scalable data modelling techniques and software quality practices
    commonly found in modern enterprise systems.

    Marks will be awarded for functionality, code quality, scalability considerations, testing, performance optimisation and
    overall solution design.
