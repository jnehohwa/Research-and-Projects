## Assumptions and limitations

- Programme or employer sponsorship statements are not legal determinations. Permit eligibility depends on duties, salary, contract, qualifications and rules in force when applying.
- EY permits only one graduate application and allows second and third preferences.
- SIG gives no salary or work-permit statement on the public vacancy.
- AIB and Bank of Ireland confirm relevant programme areas but not a live 2027 vacancy or sponsorship position as of the access date.
